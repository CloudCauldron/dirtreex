## Additional boundaries

- Public API and leaders: api-capture, api-leaders and capture-contracts cover all six constructors, live verbatim input, icons, token binding, empty fields and sequential trees.
- Refactoring controls: elbow-contracts and geometry-contracts protect caller-specific clamping and frame/row geometry.
- Integration/lifecycle: integration-wrapper-hooks, fitting-ownership, lifecycle-fit-boundaries, lifecycle-owned-state, lifecycle-inner-direct and lifecycle-zero-tail protect copied trials, +/-1sp admission boundaries, callback effects and zero-extent tails.
- Static topology: topology-lookup-contracts and topology-owner-lifetime preserve future-owner identities, ordering, callback lifetime and cleanup across long, short and empty trees.
- Finite fallback: fallback-bare-minimum and fallback-framed-closing retain every visible entry and shipped effect when a minimum row or bottom padding exceeds the column; their exact overflow diagnostics are intentional.
- The root dirtreex_examples.tex gallery covers the public feature combinations and remains the 44-page integration control.

## Acceptance rules

Successful isolated cases stabilize within two passes; the clean gallery within three. Compare marker counts/page bounds, every extracted word coordinate, RGB hashes, auxiliary bytes, shipped effects and selected contract traces. Root/font traces were added from prior accepted logs without changing other observations.

Ordinary cases require zero overfull/underfull boxes and no unresolved-reference or missing-glyph diagnostics. Warning controls require their exact recorded diagnostics: the historical Large-font case and two deliberately impossible-size fallbacks. Ten fatal controls require one dedicated error and exactly twenty unconsumed attempts. A missing expected warning/error also fails.

Golden observations are reviewed evidence, not universal correctness proofs. Pixel and numeric geometry references depend on the recorded fonts/TeX/Poppler versions. S1 remains unsupported; alternative output routines and authentic historical-format qualification are outside this baseline.

See README.md for commands and the separate reference-review workflow. The package source is unchanged by suite integration.
